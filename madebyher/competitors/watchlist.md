## Watchlist

## iTokri
- First noted: 2026-08-23
- Positioning: Inventory-based craft marketplace (buys stock from artisans), 500+ clusters, 700-800 SKUs, strong artisan storytelling, 75% revenue share to artisans
- Last change observed: 2026-08-23 — Added 150 artisans in last year; 15,000 orders/month; launched clothing line (casual wear); onboarded to ONDC (Jul 2024) — source: YourStory Jul 2025, ONDC press release Jul 2024
- Notes: Gwalior-based, founded 2012 by Nitin & Jia Pamnani. Exports to US/UK/ME/Europe. Inventory model gives quality/margin control vs pure marketplace. Expanding beyond home decor into apparel.

## Okhai
- First noted: 2026-08-23
- Positioning: Women-artisan-first platform, NGO-origin (Tata Chemicals), now standalone business. 30,000+ artisans, 15+ crafts, 20+ states. Strong physical retail push.
- Last change observed: 2026-08-23 — 30K+ artisans (up from 24K FY22 target); 67% turnover increase FY21→FY22; Kalaghoda Mumbai store opened Oct 2024; planning more collab stores; onboarded to ONDC (Jul 2024) — source: YourStory 2024, Okhai.org, ONDC press release Jul 2024
- Notes: Artisans earn ₹20K+/month. 13 standalone outlets + retail partnerships. Targeting 1L artisans. Strong "woman artisan" brand equity.

## Gaatha
- First noted: 2026-08-23
- Positioning: Principled craft documentation → marketplace. 75% revenue to artisans. High content authority, low commercial traction.
- Last change observed: 2026-08-23 — Onboarded to ONDC (Jul 2024) — source: ONDC press release Jul 2024, Sankalp Forum 2024
- Notes: Founded 2009 by NID graduates. "Integrity and growth stubbornly difficult to reconcile" (Sankalp Forum). Strong on craft storytelling/provenance pages; commercial conversion weaker.

## Jaypore
- First noted: 2026-08-23
- Positioning: Curated premium craft marketplace. Acquired by Aditya Birla Fashion & Retail (2019), absorbed into ethnic wear portfolio.
- Last change observed: 2026-08-23 — No independent activity post-acquisition; operates as ABFRL brand — source: Sankalp Forum 2024
- Notes: Validated demand for curated crafts but couldn't sustain independent growth. Now retail inventory channel.

## MeMeraki
- First noted: 2026-08-23
- Positioning: Culture-tech platform, high-ticket original artworks (₹10K–₹5.25L). 10,000+ pieces. Founder Yosha Gupta (World Bank, Gates Foundation, IDEO background).
- Last change observed: 2026-08-23 — No major public moves recently — source: Sankalp Forum 2024
- Notes: Highest ASP in segment. "Access to income is most powerful driver of inclusion" thesis. Different tier from mass-market handmade.

## GoCoop
- First noted: 2026-08-23
- Positioning: B2B2C marketplace connecting 80,000+ artisans across 350 cooperatives in 70 handloom clusters. ERP-based supply chain, training, merchandising, logistics.
- Last change observed: 2026-08-23 — No major public moves recently — source: YourStory 2024
- Notes: Bengaluru-based, founded 2014 by Siva Devireddy. Strong on handloom clusters, cooperative model.

## ExclusiveLane
- First noted: 2026-08-23
- Positioning: Handmade home decor direct from rural artisans. 10,000+ artisans. Indian Ecommerce Awards 2026 #3.
- Last change observed: 2026-08-23 — Award recognition 2026 — source: Indian Ecommerce Awards 2026
- Notes: Founded 2012 by Gautam Raj Anand. Terracotta, wooden kitchenware focus. Conscious consumer positioning.

## ONDC Ecosystem (Platform-Level Shift)
- First noted: 2026-08-23
- Positioning: Open Network for Digital Commerce — government-run interoperable e-commerce network. Artisan platforms now discoverable across all ONDC buyer apps.
- Last change observed: 2026-08-23 — 300 sellers + 400 women entrepreneurs onboarded (Jul 2024); 50 GI-tagged sellers integrated; key platforms onboarded: iTokri, Okhai, Rangsutra, Gulaab Jaipur, Very Much India, Gaon Se, Kalapuri, Iraaloom — source: ONDC press release Jul 2024, ET Retail Jul 2024
- Notes: Changes discovery dynamics — platform exclusivity eroding. MadeByHer should evaluate seller app integration. SIDBI/NABARD backing for SHGs. eSaras (NRLM) live with 800+ SHG items.

## Craftsvilla
- First noted: 2026-08-23
- Positioning: Large Indian marketplace for ethnic/handmade goods. Broad selection across price points. Moderate seller policies.
- Last change observed: 2026-08-23 — No major public moves recently — source: Quora/NovelWebSolution 2024
- Notes: Good discoverability for apparel, home decor, jewelry. Generalist vs curated.

## Fabindia (Adjacent — Large Retailer)
- First noted: 2026-08-23
- Positioning: Established retail brand with handloom basics, easy returns, physical stores nationwide. Not artisan-direct but sets consumer expectations.
- Last change observed: 2026-08-23 — No major public moves recently — source: VeeThreads 2024
- Notes: Benchmark for returns policy, physical presence, brand trust. Different model (brand-owned vs marketplace).

## DTDC "Kaarigo" (New Entrant — Logistics-Led Platform)
- First noted: 2026-09-04
- Positioning: Logistics major (16,500+ touchpoints, 220 countries) launches dedicated artisan e-commerce platform bundling storefronts, seller onboarding, cataloguing, fulfilment.
- Last change observed: 2026-09-04 — Platform launch Sep 3 2026; MoU with Uttar Pradesh govt for ODOP program; targeting 1,000 artisans onboarded in 12 months; "GI Man of India" Dr. Rajni Kant at launch — source: Entrepreneur India Sep 3 2026
- Notes: Not Bihar/ONDC-specific yet (UP-focused pilot), but well-funded logistics-backed entrant competing for same artisan supply pool. Watch for Bihar/GI-tagged-product expansion.
