# MadeByHer Strategy

## North Star + 90-day Goal
**Goal:** Establish MadeByHer as the authoritative source for GI-tagged Bihar luxury crafts.
**Baseline:** Organic clicks to Bawan Buti and Patharkatti guides (~27 clicks/mo).
**Target:** 3x organic sessions to luxury craft guides via focused "Buying Guide" content.

## Positioning & Differentiation
| Competitor | Their Claim | Our Proof | Our Edge | Message |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Generic Gifting (e.g. BoxUp) | Curated hampers | Regional artisan stories | Cultural Provenance | Not just a gift, a piece of Bihar's heritage. |
| Etsy/Amazon Handmade | Wide variety | GI-tagged authentic crafts | Direct Artisan Link | Authentic, GI-certified, maker-verified. |

## Audience Segments
- **Luxury Gifting:** Searching for "52 buti saree", "luxury handmade gifts".
- **Cultural Explorers:** Searching for "Bihar crafts", "Madhubani art".
- **Festival Shoppers:** Searching for "Chhath puja decoration", "Diwali handmade gifts".

## Content Pillars
1. **Luxury Provenance:** (Evidence: GSC queries for 52 Buti/Patharkatti). Channel: Blog/Pinterest. KPI: Organic clicks to guides.
2. **The Maker's Journey:** (Evidence: Growth Engine doc). Channel: Instagram Reels. KPI: Saves/Shares.
3. **Festival Rituals:** (Evidence: Festival Calendar/GSC Chhath queries). Channel: Blog/Social. KPI: Conversion to festival products.
4. **Authentic Gifting:** (Evidence: Market trends for "personalized" vs "authentic"). Channel: Pinterest/Blog. KPI: Referrals to shop.

## Channel Plan
- **Instagram:** 3-4x week. Focus: Maker stories, product hooks.
- **Pinterest:** High priority. Boards for "Bihar Luxury Crafts", "Handmade Diwali Gifts".
- **Blog:** SEO-driven buying guides for GI crafts.
