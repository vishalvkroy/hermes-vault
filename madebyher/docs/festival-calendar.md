## What "active" should actually produce

Not just a blog post. Check the campaign-workflow skill's channel
priorities too - Pinterest boards timed to the festival, a LinkedIn/social
post about sellers prepping stock, and the blog SEO angle (e.g. "Diwali
gift ideas under ₹1000 handmade" style long-tail). One festival, multiple
channels, all landing before the date - not same-day.
