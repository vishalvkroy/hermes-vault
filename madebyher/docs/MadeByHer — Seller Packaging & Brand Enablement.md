
> **Core idea:**  
> Women already know how to make amazing products. MadeByHer should help them turn those products into **retail-ready brands**.

---

## 1. The Problem

Many women-led small businesses are good at **making the product**, but struggle with everything that comes after manufacturing:

- Packaging
    
- Product labels
    
- Branding
    
- Stickers
    
- Product information
    
- Thank-you cards
    
- QR codes
    
- Shipping packaging
    
- Product photography
    
- Printing
    
- Finding reliable packaging vendors
    
- Understanding what information needs to go on a package
    
- Maintaining a consistent brand identity
    

This creates a gap:

> **They can make the product, but they don't always know how to package and present it like a professional brand.**

MadeByHer can solve this.

---

# 2. The MadeByHer Packaging Studio

Create a MadeByHer service called:

## **MadeByHer Packaging Studio**

The goal is simple:

> **Seller makes the product. MadeByHer helps make it retail-ready.**

Instead of asking every seller to figure everything out themselves, MadeByHer provides templates, suppliers, packaging options and eventually an ordering system.

# 4. Packaging Should NOT Make Every Seller Look the Same

This is extremely important.

MadeByHer should create a **common ecosystem**, not a generic MadeByHer private-label appearance.

The seller's brand should remain the hero.

For example:

## FRONT

**MAA KI RASOI**

Traditional Bihari Thekua

_Made with a family recipe_

---

## BACK

**Made by:** Maa Ki Rasoi  
**From:** Aurangabad, Bihar

[Product information]

### Meet the Maker

[QR CODE]

---

The MadeByHer identity can remain subtle.

For example:

> **Discover the maker → MadeByHer**

The customer should feel that they purchased from **Maa Ki Rasoi**, while MadeByHer helped them discover the brand.

# 10. Don't Build a Packaging Factory

Initially, MadeByHer should NOT manufacture everything itself.

Instead:

### MadeByHer

= Design + Standardization + Vendor Network + Ordering

### External vendors

= Printing + Manufacturing + Packaging production

This keeps the initial investment low.

---

# 11. Bulk Purchasing Advantage

A single seller might order:

> 100 stickers

and get a poor per-unit price.

MadeByHer can aggregate demand:

> 20 sellers × 500 stickers

or

> 50 sellers × 1,000 labels

This creates bulk purchasing power.

MadeByHer can negotiate with:

- Sticker printers
    
- Packaging manufacturers
    
- Box manufacturers
    
- Pouch suppliers
    
- Paper/card printers
    
- Courier packaging suppliers
    

The savings can then partially be passed to sellers.

---

# 18. Potential Revenue Model

Packaging should initially be an **enablement service**, not the primary business model.

Possible revenue:

### Option A — Margin

Buy packaging in bulk and sell to sellers with a small margin.

### Option B — Design Fee

Basic templates free.

Custom packaging design paid.

### Option C — Packaging Subscription

For high-volume sellers:

> ₹X/month

Includes a certain quantity of:

- Labels
    
- Cards

### Option D — Premium Brand Setup

MadeByHer handles:

- Logo
    
- Brand identity
    
- Packaging
    
- Product photography
    
- Seller page
    
- Product descriptions

# 19. MVP — What to Build First

Do NOT build the complete system immediately.

Start manually.

## Phase 1 — ₹0–₹10K

Create:

- 3 packaging templates
    
- 3 label templates
    
- Thank-you card
    
- Maker story card
    
- QR system
    
- Packaging checklist
    

Test with 3–5 sellers.

---

## Phase 2

Find:

- 1 sticker printer
    
- 1 box supplier
    
- 1 pouch supplier
    
- 1 paper/card printer
    

Get sample products.

Test:

- Cost
    
- Quality
    
- Minimum order quantity
    
- Delivery time
    
- Printing quality
    

---

## Phase 3

Launch:

# **MadeByHer Packaging**

Allow sellers to request packaging through WhatsApp/dashboard.

Keep the fulfillment manual initially.

---

## Phase 4

Add:

# **Order Packaging**

to the seller dashboard.

Seller selects quantity → pays → MadeByHer coordinates production → packaging delivered.

---

## Phase 5

Build:

# **MadeByHer Packaging Generator**

Seller enters product information → system generates label + QR + maker card → seller downloads print-ready files.

The most important thing:

> **Don't try to make women fit into MadeByHer. Build MadeByHer so it fits around what these women already know how to do.**

They know how to cook.

They know how to crochet.

They know how to make jewellery.

They know how to create.

They may not know packaging, branding, SEO, photography, logistics or digital marketing.
