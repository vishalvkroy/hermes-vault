# MadeByHer Content Decisions Log

Append-only. Every blog post, social post, and campaign must carry an evidence receipt
here (or in its own campaign plan.md) per the growth-strategy skill:

```markdown
## Why this (evidence receipt)
- Strategy pillar: <pillar name from strategy.md>
- Research basis: <research/analysis file + date> - <the finding, with the real number>
- Differentiator: <competitor from watchlist> - <how this piece shows we are better>
- Success metric: <metric> checked on <date> (from GSC/GA4/provider analytics)
```

No receipt = do not publish.
