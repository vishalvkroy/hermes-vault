# MadeByHer Deep Research — 2026-09-24

## High-Impact Opportunity: Pan-India "Nostalgia" Gifting for Thekua
**Real Demand Signal:** GSC data shows "pedakiya sweet bihar" (195 impressions) and "sudha thekua price" (17 impressions) as active search queries. External market data confirms Thekua is the only regional sweet recording sales in every Indian state and UT, driven by migrants in West Bengal, Maharashtra, and Delhi [1].
**The Move:** Launch a "Taste of Home" premium gift box specifically targeting the Bihari diaspora in metros. Position as "Traditional Chhath sweetness, delivered pan-India."

---

## 1. Search Intent & Traffic Analysis
- **Brand Strength:** Strong direct intent for "madebyher" (81 clicks) and "made by her" (21 clicks).
- **Product Interest:** 
    - **Traditional Sweets:** Interest in "pedakiya" and "thekua" indicates a gap for authentic, home-made Bihari sweets [GSC].
    - **Crochet:** Queries for "3d rose bag crochet" (9 impressions) suggest interest in aesthetic, floral crochet accessories.
- **Traffic Gaps:** GA4 data unavailable this run (API Error 400).

## 2. Trending in the Niche
### A. Crochet & Handmade Accessories
- **Personalization:** High demand for customizable crochet gifts (initials, custom colors) for weddings and festivals [2].
- **Top Trends 2026:** 
    - **Everlasting Florals:** Crochet flower bouquets are the #1 gift choice as permanent home decor [5].
    - **Baby Shower Gifts:** Shift toward non-toxic, handmade amigurumi toys (frogs, penguins) over plastic [4].
    - **Aesthetic Accessories:** Sunflower bandanas and floral hair accessories are trending for "slow fashion" buyers [3].

### B. Traditional Food & Gifting
- **Thekua Revolution:** Transition from a religious offering to a D2C brand. Successful models (e.g., ₹75cr brand) emphasize "home kitchen" authenticity and pan-India shipping [6].
- **Wellness Shift:** "Guilt-free" indulgence is rising; protein bars are competing with traditional sweets during festivals [1].

## 3. Festival & Market Calendar
- **Upcoming Key Date:** **Chhath Puja — November 15, 2026** [7].
- **Lead-up:** Diwali (November 8, 2026) acts as the primary gifting trigger.
- **Opportunity:** The gap between Diwali and Chhath (approx. 1 week) is the prime window for "sacred offering" and "family tradition" themed products.

## 4. Competitor Moves
- **Premiumization:** Competitors like Fabindia and IGP are shifting from "transactional mithai" to "storytelling and provenance" [8].
- **Niche Players:** Small D2C brands (e.g., Mai Mithaas, Desi Tesi) are scaling by making regional snacks available year-round, not just during festivals [9].
- **Corporate Gifting:** Shift toward sustainable, eco-friendly hampers (copperware, jute potlis) for HR/Procurement [10].

## Competitive Edge
**Gap:** Most competitors focus on "premium regional" or "corporate sustainable." There is a gap for **"Micro-Story Gifting"** — where the package includes a deep-dive into the specific woman maker's life (linking to the MadeByHer "Meet the Maker" vision).
**Concrete Move:** For the Chhath/Diwali window, create a "Maker-First Bundle" (e.g., Thekua + Crochet Accessory) where the packaging is a physical "story card" with a QR code leading to the maker's journey.
