# MadeByHer Deep Research — 2026-09-24

## High-Impact Opportunity: The Chhath Diaspora Gifting Wave
**Signal:** GSC shows rising interest in "pedakiya sweet bihar" (195 impressions, low CTR) and "sudha thekua price". GoKwik data confirms thekua is the only regional sweet ordered across every Indian state, with peak demand in Maharashtra, West Bengal, and Delhi [1][2][3].
**Action:** Launch "authentic home-kitchen" gifting hampers targeting migrant populations in metro hubs. Contrast "commercial vegetable oil" vs. "pure ghee/wooden mold" authenticity to justify premium pricing [1].

## Market Signals & Trends
### Gifting & Festival Calendar (Q4 2026)
- **Dussehra (Oct 20):** High-ticket corporate boxes [4].
- **Karwa Chauth (Oct 29):** Jewelry and "pamper" gifting [4].
- **Diwali (Nov 6-8):** Peak luxury hampers and sweets [4][5].

- **Opportunity:** Pivot crochet storytelling from "cute/hobby" to "authentic sustainable luxury" and "Granny Chic" to align with global fashion movements [7].
