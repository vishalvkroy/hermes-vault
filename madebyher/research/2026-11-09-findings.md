# MadeByHer Research Findings — 2026-11-09

## Top Opportunity: The "Giftable Authentic" Gap
Search data shows real demand for specific, high-intent Bihar crafts like **52 Buti Saree** (Nalanda) and **Patharkatti Stone Craft**. While general "handmade gifts" are a broad market, the specific regional GI-tagged luxury items are seeing direct search intent. 

**Move:** Create "Collector's Guides" or "Buying Guides" for these specific crafts (similar to the Bawan Buti guide) to capture top-of-funnel luxury gifting search.

## 1. Search Intent Analysis
- **Direct Brand Demand:** High intent for "madebyher" and "made by her" (171 combined clicks).
- **High-Intent Craft Queries:**
    - **52 Buti Saree:** Real search volume for "52 buti saree nalanda" and "52 buti saree". This is a high-ticket, luxury gifting opportunity.
    - **Patharkatti Stone Craft:** "patharkatti stone craft" is seeing organic impressions and clicks.
    - **Thekua:** "online thekua order" shows direct purchase intent.
- **Occasion-Based Search:** "chhath ghat decoration ideas" and "chhath puja decoration" are active, linking the brand to Bihar's most important festival.

## 2. Market & Trend Signals
- **Handmade Growth:** Asia-Pacific is the largest handmade market (~$578B in 2026), driven by domestic consumption [1].
- **Trending Categories:**
    - **Amigurumi/Crochet:** Global trend for "Amigurumi dolls" and handcrafted yarn goods remains strong [2].
    - **Resin Art:** Jewelry and decorative resin pieces continue to trend for personalized gifting [2].
- **Functional Home Goods:** Shift toward "everyday functional home goods" that solve daily problems, creating repeat demand [3].

## 3. Competitor Moves (Inferred)
- **Curated Gift Boxes:** Competitors like *The Zappy Box* and *BoxUp* focus on "curated gift hampers" and "personalized gift boxes" [4]. 
- **Gap:** Most competitors offer generic personalization. MadeByHer's edge is **cultural provenance** (e.g., not just a box, but a box of Madhubani art from a specific village).

## Competitive Edge
**Gap:** Competitors provide curation; MadeByHer provides *provenance*.
**Worth Closing?** Yes.
**Move:** Shift from "Gift Boxes" to "Provenance Boxes" — every box includes a "Maker's Story" card (as per the Packaging Studio doc) and a QR code to the artisan's video.
