## 1. Market Signals — Indian Handicrafts & Bihar Focus

### 1.1 Macro Market Size & Growth
| Metric | Value | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| India handicrafts market (2025) | US$ 4.85B | IBEF/IMARC (Jul 2026) |
| Projected 2034 | US$ 8.29B | CAGR 6.13% (2026-34) |

| Jewelry share of handicrafts demand | 27.84% | Credence Research (2024) |
| Top export destination | USA (38.69%) | EPCH FY2024-25 |

**Key drivers:** E-commerce accessibility, travel/tourism surge, eco-conscious consumers, government initiatives (Make in India, Bunkar & Karigar Utthan Programme launched Jul 2025 across 100 clusters, 56 training programs for 1,527 weavers).

### 1.2 Bihar-Specific Handicrafts Export Landscape
**GI-tagged handicrafts from Bihar (13 as of Jun 2026):**
- Madhubani/Mithila Painting (2007) — GI-protected, global collector base
- Appliqué/Khatwa Work (2008)
- Sujani Embroidery (2008) — Bhusura, Muzaffarpur
- Sikki Grass Products (2008) — Mithila region
- Bhagalpur Silk (2013)
- **NEW Jun 2026:** Bawan Buti Weaving, Patharkatti Stone Craft, Pidiya Painting (3 granted same day — signals active state-level documentation push)

**Export process:** IEC (1-3 days) → EPCH RCMC (7-15 days, ₹8,850) → GI authorization (1-4 weeks for Madhubani/Sikki/Sujni). HS code selection critical: Madhubani originals HS 9701.10 (0% EU/US duty), reproductions HS 4901.99 (dutiable).

**Challenges:** Most Bihar artisans sell via intermediaries at fraction of international prices; color-fastness failures on natural dyes; substandard packaging; CITES compliance for wood.

## 4. SEO Gaps — Marketplace & Artisanal E-commerce

### 4.1 Structural Gaps for Marketplaces
| Gap | Impact | Fix |
|-----|--------|-----|
| **Dual-sided keyword strategy missing** | Most optimize only for buyer terms ("buy handmade jewelry"), ignore seller terms ("sell handmade crafts") | Map both intent funnels; create seller-acquisition content hub |
| **Duplicate content at scale** | Vendor-uploaded descriptions create near-dupe pages; faceted filters multiply URLs | Canonical tags, noindex low-value facets, enforce unique description rules |
| **Vendor page authority diluted** | Individual artisan pages don't accumulate link equity | Build artisan profile hubs with schema, internal linking, UGC (reviews, stories) |
| **Vernacular/regional keyword gap** | English-only misses "मधुबनी पेंटिंग खरीदें", "छठ पूजा सामग्री" | Hinglish + regional language keyword research; schema for local business |
| **Visual search unoptimized** | Pinterest Lens, Google Lens growing; no image SEO audit | Alt text, structured data (ImageObject), Pinterest Rich Pins validation |

### 4.2 Keyword Pyramid for New/Low-Authority Domains (per Madsun Media)
| Layer | Timeline | KD | Volume | Example |
|-------|----------|-----|--------|---------|
| Foundation | Mo 1-6 | <20 | 100-500 | "madhubani painting buy online bihar artisan" |
| Growth | Mo 4-6 | 20-40 | 500-2K | "handmade chhath gifts delivery mumbai" |
| Authority | Mo 7-12 | 40-60 | 2K-10K | "buy madhubani art online" |
| Compounding | Mo 12-18 | 60+ | 10K+ | "handmade gifts india" |

**Required execution:** 4-8 quality pieces/month, active link building (craft blogs, GI authority sites, EPCH), technical soundness.

### 4.3 Competitor SEO Benchmarks (Observed)
- **iTokri:** Strong on "iTokri [craft]" branded + long-tail craft terms; artisan story pages rank well
- **Okhai:** Ranks for "okhai [product]" + "women artisan [craft]"; NGO-origin trust signals
- **Gaatha:** High authority on craft documentation pages; commercial pages weaker
- **Jaypore:** Post-acquisition, ranks via Aditya Birla domain authority; curated collection pages
- **MeMeraki:** High-ticket art keywords ("original indian artwork buy"); low volume, high value
