# MadeByHer Research Findings — 2026-10-10

## High-impact opportunity: Glam21 lip balm converting at 75% CTR, and "best papad brands in india" is a real-demand query with NO matching product

28-day GSC (sc-domain:madebyher.in) shows the page
`/product/product-name-glam21-love-tinted-lip-balm` at 3 clicks / 4 impressions, 75% CTR, average
position 1.25 — the single best-converting page on the whole site this period — driven by the
exact-match query `glam 21 love swipe lip balm brownie fudge` (2 clicks / 3 impressions) and
`glam 21 lip balm` (1/1).[1] At the same time `best papad brands in india` (1 click / 18
impressions) and `papad brands` (1/19) are real, repeated search demand (also present in the
2026-10-01 and 2026-10-05 snapshots, so this is not one-off noise[1]) landing on
`/blog/top-papad-brands-india-where-homemade-fits` (4 clicks / 280 impressions, 1.4% CTR,
position 8.5 over 28 days) — a blog post about papad that does not sell papad.[1] `admin_search_products`
confirms there is no papad product in the live catalog.[1] This is a bigger and more concrete gap
than last week's crochet-potli angle: it is real, repeated purchase-intent search traffic with
literally nothing to sell into.

**Concrete move this week:** (a) tighten the Glam21 lip balm page/meta further and consider a
small Pinterest pin set for it — it is already proving out at 75% CTR off a tiny base, worth
scaling; (b) flag to Vishal that `best papad brands in india` / `papad brands` (18+19 impressions,
28d, two separate runs now) is real demand with no sellable product — either source a papad
seller for the marketplace or stop investing blog real estate in a query the catalog can't serve.
Both recommendations follow directly from the GSC query/page data above, not from assumption.[1]

## 2. Niche trends & market signals

The India gifting market is forecast at USD 816.3M in 2025, growing to USD 1,089.9M by 2034 (3.10%
CAGR) — a much lower absolute figure than the "₹61,000-65,000 crore" figure cited in last week's
research from a different source, so note the conflict explicitly: the two reports almost
certainly scope "gifting market" differently (organized/branded retail vs. the full informal +
formal market).[10] Either way, personalisation and festival-driven demand remain the consistent
theme across sources.[10]

Pinterest Predicts 2026 names "Pen Pals" (handwritten notes, snail-mail-style gifting, +45% cute
stamps, +110% snail mail gifts, +90% penpal ideas) as a named trend already used in MadeByHer's
own 2026-09-06 Diwali/Chhath Pinterest campaign (`campaigns/2026-09-06-diwali-chhath-pinterest-predicts/`)[9]
— confirms that campaign rode a real, still-live trend rather than a one-off guess, worth revisiting
for Chhath-timed content given the trend "lasts nearly 2x longer" than typical trends per
Pinterest's own claim.[9]

Reddit's r/smallbusinessindia has an active, fresh (within hours) Diwali 2026 gifting thread
discussing budget hampers starting around ₹250–499/piece and asking for "art-led" options — real,
current small-seller gifting demand chatter consistent with MadeByHer's low-ASP gift positioning,
though this specific thread is vendor-to-vendor chatter rather than consumer sentiment, so weight
it as a seller-side signal, not a buyer signal.[11]

## 3. News and articles

iTokri's Festive 2026 collection (first flagged 2026-10-01) gives concrete order-by dates in its
own release: international orders by ~16 Oct (US/UK/Canada/Europe/Australia) or ~21 Oct
(UAE/Singapore/Malaysia), domestic by ~2 Nov for Diwali.[5] This is a usable content pattern —
MadeByHer's own "order by" messaging (already partly covered in the 2026-10-02 blog post
"Dussehra to Diwali 2026: When to Order Handmade Gifts from Bihar") could add a similarly concrete
domestic cutoff date instead of a generic "order early" line, grounded in iTokri's own published
dates as the comparison point.[5]

GI-tag momentum for Bihar crafts continues to build structurally, though this week's search found
no genuinely NEW GI tag beyond the three (Bawan Buti Weaving/Nalanda, Patharkatti Stone Craft/Gaya,
Pidiya Painting/Bhojpur) already logged in the 2026-08-23 and 2026-08-30 research runs — this is
confirmed-still-current, not new, context.[6][7] A broader DD India piece (Aug 2026) reports GI
recognition can lift rural artisans' incomes 20-30% and that India now has 800+ registered GI
products with ~29,000 authorised GI users as of Jan 2025 (up from 365) — useful macro proof point
for MadeByHer's GI-tagged-product messaging (Madhubani, Sikki, Sujni, Bawan Buti) but not
brand-specific news this week.[8]

**Festival-calendar correction needed:** `docs/festival-calendar.md` currently lists Chhath Puja as
"15 Nov 2026 (Sun)" with no breakdown of its 4 days. Drikpanchang's 2026 Chhath calendar (checked
fresh today) confirms Nahay Khay on 13 Nov (Fri), Lohanda/Kharna on 14 Nov (Sat), the main Sandhya
Arghya (Chhath Puja day) on 15 Nov (Sun), and Usha Arghya/Parana on 16 Nov (Mon).[4] The existing
single-date entry is directionally correct (15 Nov is the main day) but undersells the real
4-day window — any Chhath content/campaign timing should start lead-in messaging from ~13 Nov, not
treat 15 Nov as the only relevant date. Recommend updating festival-calendar.md to carry all 4 days
next time it's touched (not done in this file, which is research-only, per the brand's file
boundaries).

## Competitive Edge

### 1. Gap: iTokri publishes concrete, dated shipping cutoffs; MadeByHer's cutoff messaging is vaguer

iTokri's Festive 2026 PR explicitly names Oct 16/21 international and Nov 2 domestic order-by
dates.[5] MadeByHer's own 2026-10-02 post already covers "when to order" but without a single hard
calendar date in the same explicit format.[5]

**Worth closing?** Yes, but it's a copy-edit, not a new campaign — low effort, directly actionable.

**Concrete move:** When the next Diwali-adjacent content is produced (blog, Pinterest, or
Instagram), include one explicit line — e.g. "order custom/made-to-order pieces by Nov 1 for Diwali
delivery" — grounded in MadeByHer's own real production/shipping lead times (check with Vishal/the
seller-ops doc for the real number before publishing a date as fact), matching iTokri's own
explicit-date pattern.[5]

### 2. Gap worth flagging, not necessarily closing this week: a real search query with no product

`best papad brands in india` / `papad brands` (18 + 19 impressions, 28d, recurring three snapshots
running now) point at buyer intent MadeByHer cannot currently fulfill — there is no papad product
in the catalog (`admin_search_products` returned zero results for "papad").[1]

**Worth closing?** Depends on seller sourcing, which is outside this agent's remit — flagging to
Vishal rather than recommending a specific move, since this needs either a new seller onboarded or
a decision to stop ranking content for a product MadeByHer doesn't sell.

**Concrete move if sourced:** a `papad` product page would have a ready-made 280-impression blog
post (`/blog/top-papad-brands-india-where-homemade-fits`, 280 impressions / 28d per the GSC pull
above) to internally link from on day one.[1]
