## 4. SEO Gaps — Marketplace & Artisanal E-commerce (Deepened)

### 4.1 Structural Gaps (Reconfirmed + New Vernacular/AI Layer)

| Gap | Impact | Fix | New 2026 Context |
|-----|--------|-----|------------------|
| **Dual-sided keyword strategy missing** | Most optimize only for buyer terms, ignore seller terms | Map both intent funnels; create seller-acquisition content hub | ONDC makes seller acquisition critical |
| **Duplicate content at scale** | Vendor-uploaded descriptions create near-dupe pages | Canonical tags, noindex low-value facets, enforce unique description rules | AI assistants (Rufus, ChatGPT) reward unique, structured content |
| **Vendor page authority diluted** | Individual artisan pages don't accumulate link equity | Build artisan profile hubs with schema, internal linking, UGC | Voice search needs structured attributes |
| **Vernacular/regional keyword gap** | English-only misses "मधुबनी पेंटिंग खरीदें", "छठ पूजा सामग्री" | Hinglish + regional language keyword research; schema for local business | **Tier-2/3 = ~50% incremental orders (Bain 2026); voice 3x faster than typing** |
| **Visual search unoptimized** | Pinterest Lens, Google Lens growing; no image SEO audit | Alt text, structured data (ImageObject), Pinterest Rich Pins validation | Pinterest AI recognizes 4B+ objects |
| **NEW: AI/LLM discovery layer** | Amazon Rufus (300M users, $12B incremental sales), ChatGPT (160M+ MAU India, 4.5x growth 2025) | Structured attributes, honest Q&A, buyer-language alignment | Clean structured data wins in both marketplace ranking AND AI answers |

### 4.3 Vernacular/Voice Search Playbook (Zane Marketing 2026)

**Harvest from:**
1. Platform autocomplete (type product, read suggestions)
2. Own search-term reports (sponsored campaign data = proven converting phrases)
3. Competitor reviews/questions (buyer language)
4. Regional/occasion language (festival names, local sizing, dish names)

**Placement discipline:**
- Visible copy: clean, human, readable
- Backend search terms: Hinglish, misspellings, ear-spellings, long conversational phrases
- At least one bullet in spoken phrasing
- Structured attributes for AI assistants (Rufus, ChatGPT)
