# MadeByHer Deep Research — 2026-09-18

**Run date:** 2026-09-18
**Scope:** Indian/Bihar handmade-goods market signals, gifting/festival trends, Pinterest opportunities, SEO gaps, competitor moves

## Executive Summary & Priority Opportunity
- **Top Opportunity:** The newly launched **"Bharat GI" banner** (MoU signed Aug 5, 2026 between Ministry of MSME and DPIIT) to onboard GI collectives onto ONDC and GeM [1]. This directly aligns with MadeByHer's authentic Bihar craft positioning (Madhubani, Sikki, Sujni, Bawan Buti). 
- **Consumer Gifting Timing:** Diwali 2026 falls on Sunday, November 8, 2026 (5-day festival Nov 6–10) [2], followed closely by Chhath Puja from November 13–16, 2026 [4]. This creates a 5-day gap between Diwali and Chhath, presenting a prime window for cross-festival gift hampers (Thekua + Madhubani Soop + Crochet/Sikki).

- **Top Brand Queries:** `"madebyher"` (71 clicks, 86 impressions, pos 1.1) and `"made by her"` (14 clicks, 99 impressions, pos 4.2) dominate branded traffic [1].

## 2. Market & Competitor Trends
- **Competitor Landscape (Thotfy & Prama):** 2026 guides emphasize low-fee onboarding, unregistered artisan support via Unique Identification Numbers, and strict artisan attribution [1, 2].
- **Festive Calendar Alignment:** Diwali (Nov 8, 2026) and Chhath (Nov 13–16, 2026) require early lead generation on Pinterest [2, 4]. Competitors are pushing bespoke hampers, terracotta, and artisanal home decor [2].

---

## 3. Competitive Edge & Actionable Gap Analysis
1. **What are competitors doing that we aren't?** Platforms like Prama and Thotfy are actively marketing "Artpreneur" and unregistered vendor onboarding pathways [1, 2], while government initiatives are pushing the **Bharat GI** framework on ONDC [1].
2. **Is that gap worth closing?** Yes. Integrating under the Bharat GI framework builds institutional trust and captures high-intent organic search for authenticated Bihar crafts.
3. **What's the concrete move?** Launch a dedicated "Bharat GI Authenticity Hub" landing page on MadeByHer targeting authentic Bihar crafts (Sikki, Madhubani, Sujni) ahead of the Diwali-Chhath window, and prepare ONDC catalog mapping under the Bharat GI guidelines.
