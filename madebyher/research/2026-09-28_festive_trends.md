# Research Findings — 2026-09-28

## High-Impact Opportunity: The "Bihar Festive Box" (Cross-Craft Bundle)
**Evidence:** 
- **Search Intent:** GSC shows high impressions for "pedakiya sweet bihar" (164 imp) and "thekua vs gujiya difference" (138 imp), but low CTR. This suggests strong curiosity about traditional Bihari sweets that isn't being satisfied by existing landing pages. [1]
- **Market Signal:** Council Review identifies a shift toward "Mixed-craft hampers" combining Madhubani coasters, Sujni runners, and Thekua tins as a single "Bihar box" SKU for urban buyers. [2]
- **Product Gap:** Most sellers offer single-category items. Bundling "Culture + Taste + Art" creates a premium gifting experience that justifies higher ASP and targets the "Heritage" aesthetic preferred by urban millennials.

**Concrete Move:**
Create a "Mithila Heritage Gift Box" for Diwali/Chhath. 
- **Contents:** 1 pack of Maa Ki Rasoi Thekua + 1 set of Madhubani coasters + 1 Sujni-inspired accent piece.
- **Packaging:** Use Madhubani motifs as the external packaging design (as suggested by market trends) to turn the box itself into a piece of art.
- **Hook:** "The Taste and Art of Mithila in One Box."

---

## 1. Search Intent & Demand Analysis
- **Rising Interest in Authentic Sweets:** "Pedakiya" and "Thekua" are driving significant impressions. There is a clear gap in providing a definitive "comparison guide" or "buying guide" that converts this curiosity into sales. [1]
- **Direct Product Intent:** Janmashtami-special "Laddu Gopal Jhula" showed a very high CTR (14%), proving that ritual-specific, time-bound products have the highest conversion rates. [1]
- **Generic vs. Specific:** Generic queries like "madebyher" are common, but the real growth is in long-tail, craft-specific queries ("applique khatwa work of bihar").

## 2. Niche Trends & Buyer Behavior
- **Aesthetic Shift:** Move from "festive kitsch" (loud red/gold) to "Heritage Minimalist" (earthy palettes: terracotta, indigo, mustard). [2]
- **Authenticity Anxiety:** Buyers struggle to distinguish hand-painted Madhubani from digital prints. Providing a "Certificate of Authenticity" or "Maker Video QR" is a critical trust-builder. [2]
- **Experience Gifting:** Rise of "DIY Kits" (e.g., DIY Thekua mix + video tutorial) for families to bond during festivals. [2]

## 3. Competitor Moves & Gaps
- **Generic Platforms:** Most competitors focus on "Handmade India" generally. MadeByHer's edge is the "Deep Bihar" focus (Mithila, Magadh, Angika regions).
- **Packaging Gap:** Competitors use standard courier packaging. Using "Retail-Ready" branded packaging that tells the maker's story creates a massive perceived value jump.

## Competitive Edge
**Gap:** Competitors lack "Cross-Category Bundling" (Food + Art + Textile).
**Is it worth closing?** Yes. High-ASP bundles increase average order value (AOV) and reduce shipping cost per item.
**Move:** Launch "Festival Bundles" (e.g., Thekua + Madhubani Art) starting Oct 1st to lead into Diwali.
