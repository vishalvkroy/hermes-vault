## Pin 1: How to spot a real Madhubani painting
**Title:** Real Madhubani painting vs. a mass-produced copy: what to check
**Description:** Genuine Madhubani art uses natural dyes (ochre, indigo, maroon), double-line borders, and motifs specific to the Mithila region: fish, peacocks, the sun and moon, ritual scenes. Mass-produced prints flatten the line work and use flat synthetic color instead. MadeByHer's Madhubani pieces come directly from women artisans in Bihar's Mithila region, GI-tagged craft, not a factory print. Look for the artisan's name on the listing before you buy.
**Link:** https://www.madebyher.in/blog/how-to-buy-authentic-madhubani-paintings-online-a-complete-buying-guide
**Hashtags/keywords:** authentic madhubani painting, real vs fake madhubani, GI tag handicraft bihar

## Pin 2: Sikki grass jewelry, the real material test
**Title:** Sikki grass jewelry: how to tell handwoven from plastic-look imitations
**Description:** Real sikki jewelry is woven from a golden marsh grass that grows in North Bihar wetlands. It's lightweight, has a natural sheen, and shows small irregularities from hand-weaving. Cheaper synthetic versions run heavier, look uniformly glossy, and don't age the same way. Every piece MadeByHer sells is hand-tied by women artisans, not machine-pressed.
**Link:** https://www.madebyher.in/blog/sikki-grass-jewelry-a-buying-guide-to-bihar-s-golden-grass-craft
**Hashtags/keywords:** sikki grass jewelry real, handwoven bihar craft, authentic grass jewelry india

## Pin 3: Sujni embroidery's storytelling stitch
**Title:** Sujni embroidery isn't just decoration. It's Bihar women stitching their own stories.
**Description:** Sujni started as a way to recycle old saris into quilts, then grew into an embroidery style where each panel tells a story: a wedding, a harvest, a piece of village life. A real Sujni piece takes weeks of hand-stitching by one artisan or a small group. Something calling itself "Sujni-style" that got machine-embroidered in a day is a different thing.
**Link:** https://www.madebyher.in/blog/sujni-embroidery-the-complete-buying-guide-to-bihar-s-storytelling-stitch
**Hashtags/keywords:** sujni embroidery bihar, authentic hand embroidery india, handmade quilt story

## Pin 4: GI tags, explained for gift buyers
**Title:** What a GI tag actually means when you're buying a handmade gift
**Description:** A Geographical Indication tag is a legal mark: a craft can only carry that name if it's genuinely made in its home region, by its traditional method. Madhubani, Sikki, and Sujni are all GI-tagged crafts from Bihar. Buying GI-tagged means the money reaches the artisan community the craft actually comes from, not a copy made somewhere else.
**Link:** https://www.madebyher.in/gifts
**Hashtags/keywords:** GI tag meaning, buy authentic handicrafts, support real artisans india
