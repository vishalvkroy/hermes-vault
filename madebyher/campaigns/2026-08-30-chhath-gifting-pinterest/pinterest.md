## Performance+ Test Campaign (5K Budget — Week 1)

Campaign structure:
- Objective: Conversions (add-to-cart to purchase)
- Targeting: Women 25-45, India metros + diaspora pincodes, interests: festivals, handmade, gifting, Bihar culture
- Creative: 3 variants — (A) Thekua hero, (B) Metallic soop premium, (C) Artisan story montage
- Budget: 5K over 7 days, daily cap 714
- Bid strategy: Target CPA (start conservative, let Performance+ optimize)
- Measurement: Pinterest Tag + GA4 enhanced ecommerce

Creative specs:
- Video: 15-30 sec, 9:16, captions mandatory (sound-off default)
- Static: 2:3 ratio, text overlay under 20%, brand logo bottom-right
- Idea Pin: 5-10 frames, first frame hooks with "Chhath prep starts now"

Success threshold: CPA under 800 (vs manual campaign benchmark 975 based on 18% Performance+ advantage)
