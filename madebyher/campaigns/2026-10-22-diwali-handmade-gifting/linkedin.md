36. That's how many handmade Diwali gifts are live on MadeByHer right now, across 15 women-led Bihar sellers.

Last month a new competitor showed up on our own flagship product: The Thekua Company, a platform built on one idea, one woman, one recipe, one brand. They already have live customer reviews, size-tier packaging, and a running festive discount code.

We don't sell one recipe. We sell a full shelf, thekua next to Madhubani coasters next to a crochet potli bag, because gifting rarely happens one item at a time. It happens as a hamper.

Diwali lands in 17 days, the single busiest week of our year. Our own thekua page picked up its first real clicks this month, 0 to 2, after sitting at zero for weeks despite ranking near the top of page one.

Small number. But it moved before we touched the page.

We're not copying their platform shape. We're copying the one thing that's clearly working for them: a visible, time-bound discount, scoped to one product so it doesn't dilute the rest of the shelf.

If you sell handmade this festival season, which matters more: being the best at one product, or being the easiest place to build a whole hamper?
