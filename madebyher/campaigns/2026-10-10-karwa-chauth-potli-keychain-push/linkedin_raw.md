3 weeks. 13 and 17. Zero.

Those are the real numbers: for three straight weeks, Google Search Console showed two of our product pages getting found. "Crochet rose potli bag" - 13 impressions in the last 7 days. "Daisy flower keychain" - 17, up from 11 the week before. Real people searching, real exact-match phrasing, landing on real product pages.

Zero clicks. Both weeks. Both pages.

We talked about it in last week's research notes. We talked about it the week before that too. It sat in the backlog as "Exp 5: product SEO + Pinterest push" while we shipped other things that felt more urgent - a festival post, a competitor response, a seller-acquisition push. Every one of those was a reasonable call in isolation. Three weeks running the same unshipped line was not.

What finally moved it: Karwa Chauth is 19 days out, and both products are a genuine fit for it - a handmade potli bag and a crochet keychain are exactly the kind of small, personal gift people search for the week before. So this week's campaign isn't a generic festival post. It's the overdue fix, wearing a festival as an excuse to finally happen.

I don't think the lesson here is "ship faster." It's that a number sitting in a weekly report for three runs in a row should outrank whatever feels urgent that week, and we let it sit instead.
