# Campaign Plan: Pinterest Chhath Gifting Launch

**Campaign ID:** 2026-08-23-pinterest-chhath-gifting
**Date:** 2026-08-23
**Status:** drafted

## Objective
Build Pinterest presence for MadeByHer around Chhath gifting collections — capturing high-intent festival shoppers 6-8 weeks before the festival (Chhath typically falls in Oct/Nov). Drive traffic to product pages and build board authority before peak planning season.

## Hypothesis
Pinterest's 96% unbranded search share and 4x ROAS vs other platforms, combined with zero local handmade competition on the platform, means early board/pin investment for Chhath-specific keywords (thekua gift box, madhubani soop, sikki jewelry, metallic soop) will capture disproportionate discovery traffic vs competitors who launch late.

## Target Audience
- Primary: Women 25-45 in India (metro + tier 2) planning Chhath gifts — searching "chhath gifts for mother", "thekua gift box online", "madhubani soop buy"
- Secondary: Bihari diaspora (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, US/UK/Canada/Australia) sending gifts home — searching "chhath gifts delivery india", "bihari handicrafts online"
- Tertiary: Gen Z (18-25) discovering tradition via "thrift flip" aesthetic — searching "vintage madhubani", "upcycled sikh grass", "aesthetic chhath decor"

## Research Finding This Responds To
From 2026-08-23-deep-research.md Opportunity Scoring:
1. **Pinterest organic + shoppable Pins** (Score 9, IMMEDIATE) — 96% unbranded search, 4x ROAS, zero local handmade competition, Gen Z Bihari diaspora
2. **Chhath-specific gifting collections** (Score 9, IMMEDIATE pre-Oct) — ₹12-50K cr market, Thekua national trend, metallic soop premium, early planning gap (brands launch too late)

## Channels Used
1. **Pinterest** (primary) — 5 starter boards, 15-20 pins with Rich Pin validation, shoppable product tags
2. **Instagram** (secondary) — 3 Reels + 5 carousel posts cross-promoting Pinterest boards, artisan process content
3. **SEO** (secondary) — 3 collection landing pages targeting Foundation-layer keywords, schema markup for Product + LocalBusiness

## Success Metrics (Phase 4 will fill)
- Pinterest: monthly impressions, outbound clicks, saves, shoppable Pin clicks, board followers
- Instagram: Reels views, profile visits from Pinterest link-in-bio, referral traffic to site
- SEO: organic sessions to collection pages, keyword rankings for Foundation-layer terms, conversion rate

## Timeline
- Week 1 (this week): Board setup, pin creation, Rich Pin validation, collection page drafts
- Week 2-3: Pin scheduling, Reels production, schema implementation
- Week 4-6: Amplify top performers, add video pins, UGC solicitation
- Week 6-8 (pre-Chhath): Peak promotion, gift guide pins, last-minute shipping messaging

Organic-only this cycle. Paid Pinterest (via Aleph India) evaluation for next cycle based on organic traction.
