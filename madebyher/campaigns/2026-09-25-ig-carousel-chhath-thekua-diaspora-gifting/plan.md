# Campaign: 2026-09-25-ig-carousel-chhath-thekua-diaspora-gifting

**Objective:** Drive Chhath-window gifting orders (Nov 13-16, festival active per 21-day rule
from ~Oct 23) via one Instagram carousel targeting Bihar diaspora gift-buyers searching for
authentic thekua.

**Hypothesis:** A carousel that shows the real home-kitchen process (wooden mold, ghee, no
factory line) alongside real product photos and prices will earn saves/shares from diaspora
buyers who are already searching but not converting (low CTR despite real impressions).

**Target audience:** Segment 1 from strategy.md - Bihar/Purvanchali diaspora gift-buyers,
25-45, metro India + abroad, already searching "pedakiya sweet bihar" / "sudha thekua price."

**Channels:** Instagram (@madebyher.1n) - single carousel, 7 slides.

**Status:** pending_approval (Instagram carousel drafted, awaiting Vishal's approval click).
