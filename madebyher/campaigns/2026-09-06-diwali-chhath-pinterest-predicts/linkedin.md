## Post copy

Diwali and Chhath are five days apart this year. Diwali is November 8, Chhath runs November 13 to 16.

Most years there's a two to three week gap between them. Brands plan two separate campaigns: wind down Diwali, then ramp up Chhath. This year that gap almost disappears.

For a small women-led marketplace like ours, that's actually useful. It means one production run and one gifting collection can cover both festivals instead of two. Diyas and Madhubani decor for Diwali, thekua and sikki soops for Chhath, same shipping window, same hampers if a customer wants both.

It also means whoever plans for one combined window instead of two separate ones gets a head start. We're building our gifting collection around that now, nine weeks out.

If you work with artisan supply chains or festival-season commerce in India, curious whether you're seeing the same compressed timing this year.
