## Objective
Get Pinterest content live now for the compressed 2026 festival window (Diwali Nov 8, Chhath Nov 13-16, only 5 days apart) by riding two named Pinterest Predicts 2026 trends - "Throwback Kid" (crochet, vintage kids fashion, nostalgia) and "Pen Pals" (handwritten notes, snail-mail-style gifting) - against MadeByHer's existing crochet, Madhubani, sikki, and thekua SKUs. Secondary: one LinkedIn post from Vishal's account framing the compressed calendar as a first-mover window, since competitors are still planning Diwali and Chhath as two separate campaigns.

## Hypothesis
Pinterest itself is telling us what will search well this season (Throwback Kid nostalgia toys +225%, Pen Pals snail mail gifts +110%). Nobody in the Bihar handmade-goods competitor set (iTokri, Okhai, Gaatha, Jaypore) has content mapped to these named trends yet. Pinning against them now, 9 weeks out, should out-rank generic "festival gift" pins once diaspora and gifting searches ramp up in October.

## Target Audience
- Primary: Women 25-45 in India and diaspora (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, US/UK/Canada) buying gifts for kids, parents, or send-home Chhath packages.
- Secondary: Gen Z/millennial Pinterest users following nostalgia/vintage aesthetics who haven't associated that look with Indian handmade crafts yet.
- Tertiary (LinkedIn): Founders, MSME/artisan-economy watchers, potential B2B/GI-program partners.
