₹25,000 for a complete small business website. One price, posted on the page, no discovery call.

That's the pitch I built Astra Studio's Services line on in September. Last week a studio in Hyderabad, FlowLaunch, started running the identical pitch: one fixed price, no quote forms, free hosting and a GitHub handover, shipped in under five days.

Good. It means fixed pricing is winning the argument against the quote-call guessing game Indian web agencies have run for years. Every 2026 pricing guide says the same thing, and so does every Reddit thread asking "how much should a website actually cost in India": buyers don't trust "request a quote," they want a number before they pick up the phone.

But a published price and fast hosting is table stakes now, not a moat. Any studio can write a price sheet.

Here's the part a hosting freebie can't match: every Astra Studio website or app project ships with three months of Astra Atlas free, a real CRM, POS and WhatsApp marketing system we built and run ourselves, not a marketing bolt-on. Starter is ₹25,000. Growth is ₹45,000. Elite with both apps is ₹1,10,000. The free months alone are worth ₹2,997, and the business keeps running on the software long after the site ships.

A website is a one-time deliverable. A system you keep using isn't.

What's the one number your last developer never gave you straight?

P.S. If FlowLaunch is reading this: competing on honest pricing is a good problem for Indian SMBs to have. Keep going.
