# RabbitLore Research Findings — 2026-09-21

## Executive Summary & Opportunity
Gen Z avatar culture has shifted from static profile pictures to dynamic, tool-assisted expressive identities (Roblox 144M DAU [1], Picsart Persona [2], TikTok 3D caricature trend [3]). The core emotional driver remains the overthinker's need for a safe space ("I thought I was the only one" [10]). 

**Top Opportunity:** Capitalize on the "faceless creator / personal lore" wave by offering lightweight, expressive 3D avatar customisation paired with private, night-gated lore sharing, bypassing the exhaustion of live video creation.

## 2. Niche & Competitor Trends
- **Picsart Persona:** Launched March 2026, targeting the "faceless creator" economy with style-based character generation and cross-platform consistency ("create once, reuse everywhere") [2].
- **TikTok 3D Caricature Wave:** Glossy, semi-Pixar collectible-figure aesthetics used for rapid social signaling (fandom, micro-expertise, political/aesthetic micro-identity) [3].
- **ZEPETO & Roblox:** ZEPETO active through version 4.28.100 (August 2026 updates featuring live profile effects and multi-person templates) [5]. Roblox hit 144M DAU in Q4 2025 with 274M daily avatar updates [1].

---

## 3. Competitive Edge
1. **What are they doing that we aren't?** Competitors like Picsart Persona and ZEPETO focus on broad influencer tools and commercial avatar publishing. They sell external clout and mass visibility.
2. **Is that gap worth closing for us?** No. RabbitLore's positioning explicitly rejects virality, followers, and public algorithms in favor of inner-world connection and overthinker sanctuary.
3. **What's the concrete move?** Lean entirely into our "night-gated lore sharing" and private micro-community mechanics. While competitors build public influencer avatars, RabbitLore builds an introverted sanctuary where avatars represent inner thoughts and personal histories rather than performance metrics.
