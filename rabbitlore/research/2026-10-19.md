## Executive Summary & Opportunity

**Top finding this week: Coverstar's Google Trends series set a new all-time high (100 on Sep 19,
2026) and the current post-spike floor (Oct 5–19) is running noticeably higher than the pre-spike
floor — Oct 12: 14, Oct 17: 19, Oct 18: 15, Oct 19: 32 — vs the near-zero/isolated-spike pattern
seen through August.** Daily TIMESERIES (90-day window, re-pulled this run) shows the full shape:
isolated single-day spikes through Jul–Aug (36, 43, 30, 31, 26, 25, 31, 29, 27, 48 on scattered
single days, zero around them), one outsized spike to 100 on Sep 19, then a step up to a
sustained, noisy-but-nonzero daily floor from Oct 3 onward (35, 36, 13, 9, 13, 12, 14, 15, 22, 14,
11, 10, 12, 11, 19, 15, **32**). This continues — and strengthens — the "sustained climb, not
isolated spikes" read first flagged 2026-10-12 and repeated 2026-10-15. No change to the standing
Competitive Edge call (track, don't chase — Gen Alpha audience, public-video mechanic, not
RabbitLore's).

**Second finding: this run's news/web search surfaced the first non-watchlist-competitor signal in
several runs — a named, dated Gen Z behavioral trend ("Posting Zero" / "ghost posting") directly
on-thesis for RabbitLore's own positioning, plus confirmation that Meta is building a Night
Mode/curfew into Instagram and Facebook for under-18 users as part of its $18B multi-state
settlement.** Moneycontrol (Jan 8 2026) and Globe and Mail (Sep 22 2026) both independently
describe Gen Z moving public posting into private DMs, Close Friends lists, and group chats while
leaving public grids empty[5][6] — the same "private > public" macro-thesis already backing
Pillar 1, now with a second, differently-sourced article repeating the behavior description
months apart (Jan and Sep 2026), which is a stronger signal than one article alone. Separately,
Meta's Night Mode (midnight–6am blackout for under-18 users on Instagram/Facebook, expanding to
10pm–7am if TikTok/YouTube adopt matching rules) is confirmed live policy from an $18B settlement
(not the $17B figure logged 2026-10-01 — TechCrunch's own coverage states the settlement's value
as "roughly $18 billion"[3][4], correcting the earlier number)[3][4]. This is the single biggest
platform-level validation yet of RabbitLore's own night-gating mechanic: the largest social
platform in the world is now structurally restricting after-midnight teen access, the same hours
RabbitLore already gates lore-sharing around — not a competitor move to react to, but external
proof the time-boxing concept itself has real regulatory/product-design momentum behind it.

**Top Opportunity:** the "Posting Zero"/ghost-posting finding is the most citable, most audience-
relevant piece of outside validation surfaced for Pillar 1 since Yope's funding round — it names
the exact behavior RabbitLore's product already serves ("private digital corners," "close friends
lists," "DM-only sharing," moving off "the public stage") in language a Reddit post could quote
almost directly. Recommend this becomes the next Reddit devlog/postmortem angle: not "look at our
feature" but "here's what Gen Z is already doing without a product built for it" — framed as
market observation, never a Highrise/competitor callout, consistent with the "genuine contribution,
not an ad" rule already in strategy.md §5.

## 3. Competitive Edge

1. **What are they doing that we aren't?** No named watchlist competitor (Yope, Zentara,
   ZenFriend, Coverstar, Highrise/ZEPETO) shipped, raised, or reported anything materially new this
   week — Zentara and ZenFriend returned zero news results again, same null pattern as every run
   since 2026-09-18. The real "doing" this week isn't competitor-specific: it's platform-level
   (Meta's Night Mode) and category-level (the "Posting Zero" behavioral trend), both of which
   validate RabbitLore's own mechanic rather than exposing a gap to close.
2. **Is this gap worth closing for us?** Not applicable in the usual sense — there's no feature gap
   here, only external validation. Meta's Night Mode and the Posting Zero trend both point the same
   direction RabbitLore already built toward (night-gating, private-over-public sharing); nothing
   to copy, nothing to chase.
3. **What's the concrete move?** (a) Correct `strategy.md`'s review log: the Meta settlement figure
   should read ~$18B per TechCrunch's own reporting[4], not $17B as logged 2026-10-01 — note this
   as a correction, not a new fact. (b) The "Posting Zero"/ghost-posting finding is strong enough,
   independently corroborated across two dated articles 8 months apart[5][6], to cite as fresh
   macro-thesis evidence for Pillar 1 alongside Yope's funding — add to strategy.md §4 Pillar 1.
   (c) Recommend to this week's campaign-workflow run: a Reddit devlog/postmortem post built around
   the "Posting Zero" language (quoting the actual behavior shift Gen Z is already naming, not a
   competitor callout) is the best-evidenced next Reddit angle — concrete, scoped, non-promotional,
