## 2. Trending in the Niche

### Apple: 18+ minimum rating for dating & AI-companion apps — new, dated, names the exact mechanics two watchlist competitors run

Apple announced Nov 2 2026 that two new content descriptors land in App Store Connect on Nov 16
2026: "Dating and Relationships" (features that connect real people for dating or relationships,
or a virtual companion that engages the user in sexual or romantic content — minimum rating 18+)
and "Shared Location" (sharing a user's real-world location with another person or group — may or
may not change minimum rating depending on region).[1][2][3][4][5] Every app must have both
declarations completed by Jan 12 2027. TechCrunch names Character.AI and Replika as examples of
apps the companion clause could affect, and notes AI companion apps across Apple/Google app
stores had been downloaded 220M+ times globally as of last summer.[3]

Read against the watchlist: **ZenFriend** (AI virtual friend for Gen Z loneliness/anxiety) is a
plausible direct hit on the companion clause — its whole positioning is simulated-friend
companionship, the precise category the rule targets, though no source checked this run confirms
ZenFriend specifically has filed or will file a sexual/romantic-content declaration; this is a
structural-fit read, not a confirmed enforcement action against ZenFriend by name. **Highrise**
runs large, long-running, member-populated public rooms explicitly named "Find a Date" (one
checked this run shows 116 people present) and "Find a Fang," a different structural case — real
people connecting for dating inside a broader social platform's user-generated rooms, not an
AI-companion feature — but one that plausibly reads under the "connect real people for dating or
relationships" half of the same descriptor.[6] Neither read is a claim that Apple has acted on
either app specifically; both are a structural fact about the rule's stated scope matched against
each app's own public-facing room/feature design, checked this run, not inferred from older notes.

### Highrise — 5.0 beta ships, "Find a Date"/dating-room model unchanged

Highrise posted a dev diary (Nov 2 2026) announcing a 5.0 beta — a full rebuild on a custom

## Competitive Edge

**What are they doing that we aren't?** Nothing new on the "what feature should we build" axis —
this week's finding is regulatory pressure landing *on* two named competitors' existing mechanics
(ZenFriend's AI companion, Highrise's public dating rooms), not a new feature either has shipped
that RabbitLore lacks. The gap, if any, is structural exposure: ZenFriend and Highrise's core
loops touch exactly the surface Apple is now rating 18+; RabbitLore's peer-to-peer lore-matching
(`getBlend`) and night-gated private rooms have no dating feature and no AI companion, so this
specific regulatory clause has no bearing on RabbitLore's own app-store standing.

**Is that gap worth closing?** There is no gap to close here — this is not a feature RabbitLore
is missing, it's a cost landing on two competitors for features RabbitLore was never trying to
build (per `docs/positioning.md`: "Not social media. Not algorithms. Not followers. Not
virality" — and nothing about dating or AI companionship either). The real value this week is
differentiation copy, not a product decision.

**What's the concrete move?** (1) Fold this into the existing, standing non-attack "no public
room, no AI companion" structural positioning line (already used for Highrise's trust/safety and
App Store 13+ findings in `research/2026-09-28.md` and `research/2026-10-12.md`) as a 3rd,
independent structural reason — now regulatory/age-rating, not just trust-cost or compliance-13+
— that a private, no-dating, no-companion app structurally sits outside of. Do not name ZenFriend
or Highrise directly in outward copy, consistent with the standing rule. (2) Do not create a new
pillar or watchlist entry for this — it strengthens the existing Pillar 1/§2 positioning rows for
Highrise/ZEPETO and Zentara/ZenFriend, it doesn't introduce a new competitor or mechanic. (3) When
the publishing backlog finally clears, this is strong material to fold into whichever LinkedIn/
Reddit piece goes out next, alongside or after the Meta Threads Friends validation point — per
council_review's read (single model, Groq, reported honestly — Claude/Gemini/Mistral all failed
to respond this run), Meta's validation is still the stronger headline, with this week's Apple
finding as supporting differentiation, not the lead.
